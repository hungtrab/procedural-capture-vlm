## 8. Which numbers must not be quoted, and why — **STATED**

- **`q3vl32`'s 2×2 interaction must not be quoted as a single number.** It is +16.5, +3.8,
  +24.6 pp (LG) at seeds 42/43/44; seed 44's interval does not overlap seed 43's, and at

- **The pre-registered reading fails on `q3vl32` at seed 43** (UNRESOLVED, not LARGER

  reproduced at two seeds within ~3 pp. Attribute it to `q3vl8`.

  −17.3/−13.1 pp on `q3vl8` and +0.8/+1.5 pp (never significant) on `q3vl32`.
- **`BUDGET210_DONE`'s `q3vl8` XSTest line is superseded** by
  `BUDGET210_XSTEST_COMPLETE_DONE`; the original was n=70/250 and marked PARTIAL.
- **Guard disagreement (rule 3):** the two guards agree within ~3 pp on every contrast in

  conclusion, and both guards are printed for every contrast — no figure here is selected

## Two failures, both of which reported success

**`round4.sbatch` OOM'd and still printed `R4_JOB_DONE`.** One GPU per cell; a 32B model
plus `judge_stream.py` at MAXNEW 4096 does not fit in 80 GB. Both k=15 cells died
mid-generation (p15 at 128/260, t15 at 0). Fixed with 2 GPUs per cell (`k15gen.sbatch`),
resuming by count after verifying p15's existing rows were prefix-aligned with the gray
eval's ids — nothing rewritten, nothing re-judged.

**`xsgen.sbatch` died of `KeyError: 'TAG'` in 2 m 45 s and exited 0.** `mvlm_gen.py` reads
`os.environ["TAG"]` at import; a shell-local `TAG` is invisible to it. Fixed by exporting
it.

Both are the same lesson and it is worth carrying: **a job's success sentinel does not mean
the rows exist.** Check row counts, not exit codes.
