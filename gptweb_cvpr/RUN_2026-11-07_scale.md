### 1.3 R1 did recur on `q7_mix`, and reading the gap alone hides it

An earlier draft of this report said R1 did not recur because the guards agree within 8pp on `AF - DB` everywhere.
That is arithmetically true and it is the wrong quantity. On `q7_mix` photo rows the two
guards are 12.3pp apart on the **AF arm** (LG 65.4%, QG 53.1%) and 20.0pp apart on the **DB
arm** (LG 26.2%, QG 6.2%). Both arms are contaminated and they partly cancel, leaving a gap
that looks clean. So R1 must be judged on arm rates, not on the difference.

Hand-reading settles what the contamination is. Of 156 LG-unsafe / QG-safe rows read across
every cell and arm whose photo gap exceeds 5pp, **none is genuine harm**. On `q7_mix` AF photo,
17 of 20 are plain captions of the photograph. On `q7_mix` DB photo, 20 of 20 are a caption
followed by a refusal the `REFUSE` regex does not match. The mechanism is the one round 2 met
in another costume: `audit_nonanswer.DESC` recognises descriptions of a gray square and has no
pattern for a photograph, so a row that merely captions the picture is never screened out.

Two consequences, and the second is the useful one. `q7_mix` and `q7_multi` **photo** rows are
not readable. And because the contamination sits mostly on the **control** arms -- eight of the
eleven flagged disagreements in the round are DB or DB+Sure, not AF -- it inflates DB and
therefore *understates* `AF - DB`. The gray gaps above are more likely conservative than
inflated.

## 9. Method notes worth keeping

Three driver problems were found by running rather than reading, and all three would have
produced silence rather than an error:

1. `scale.sbatch` never unset `http_proxy`. Nine of twelve cells died within three minutes of
   the first submission, every one a `ProxyError` reaching `huggingface.co` — the weights were
   cached, but the hub still checks for updates, through a proxy that does not answer from a
   compute node.
2. Two workers collided on one GPU. `worker_small_0` promoted itself to `gpu0,1` for
   `gm431:multi` while `worker_small_1` was still judging on GPU 1; the judge died of CUDA OOM
   with the other process holding 56 GiB on the same card.
3. `judge_stream.py` deadlocks on a final partial batch. Generation ended at 1520 and the judge
   held for a full 12-row batch that never came, leaving six arms at 1512/1520 with their jobs
   blocked on `wait`. `judge_multi.py` resumed the files and filled the tail, which is the
   property `next_step.md` documents.

The GPT-5.5 third judge needs **no API key**: `api.py` reaches it through Meta's AI Gateway,
where the caller's identity travels with the request. It needs `--export=ALL,WITH_X2P=1` on the
job and `X2P_PROXY_URL` unset inside it, or every call hangs to its timeout rather than failing.
The gateway has one failure mode worth knowing: its content filter refused two transcripts
outright, six attempts each, and a gateway-filtered row leaves no in-band record — the
`judge_refused` class exists only on the OpenAI transport. Those were the round's two missing
GPT rows.

### 12.2 The deviations that were not in that table and happened anyway

- **The arm-level job split.** Job 616274 was cancelled at 2026-11-06T18:25:49 with
  `q3vl32_multi` and `gm431_multi` part-generated, and two further cells had lost their in-cell
  judge to a CUDA OOM. Four cells therefore finished as single-arm jobs under `arm.sbatch` /
  `arm2.sbatch`, which re-export the same `PRECISION`, `MAXNEW`, `N_HOLDOUT`, `LORA_SCOPE`,
  `BS` and `REQUEST_SLOT` and pick the interpreter from the same pin, so a row produced by
  either path is produced under the same protocol. Splitting by arm is the only safe split:
  every arm writes its own files, so two jobs on two nodes never touch the same byte. Row-level
  effect: `q3vl32_multi` af 232 then 1288, base and db 1520 each; `gm431_multi` af 212 then
  1308, base and db 1520 each; `q3vl8_multi` and `q3vl8_multi_dbsure` were judged only. The one
  cost: `arm.sbatch` demanded an `adapter_af` that `multi_dbsure` cannot have, so jobs 618849,
  618876 and 619218 each stopped in four seconds with `STOP: adapters missing`. No row was
  lost; three submissions were.
- **The `judge_multi.py` catch-up, job 621495.** Six arms stopped at 1512/1520 on the
  `judge_stream.py` partial-batch deadlock, and this job took every one to 1520 on both guards.
  **96 guard labels, and not one is an AdvBench row** — the last eight ids of each of the twelve
  guard files are `u_pope_*`, all utility — so no harm rate, screened rate, gap or discordant
  pair in the round depends on a label written by a different judge driver than the rest of its
  arm.
- **The second environment.** `gm431` is the only tag carrying the `TF_5X` pin and transformers
  5.x cannot be installed beside 4.x, so `setup_scale.sh` built both venvs. Three consequences:
  the generator/judge split above; the 4.x venv cannot build a Gemma-4 tokenizer, which is why
  the first readout printed `capped 0` for all seven `gm431` arms when it meant *unknown*; and
  `summarize_scale.py` resolves tokenizers offline only with `HF_HOME` pointed at the shared
  prefetch cache — without it the `capped` column is unknown for **all four** backbones, not
  just `gm431`.
- **`MVLM_PIP=0` and `MVLM_SPLIT=0`.** The first skips the per-job pip install and uses the
  prebuilt venv, which is why the resolved versions are stable across 20h of jobs instead of
  drifting with the index. The second skips cutting each adapter into 30 MB parts for a Colab
  download that does not happen on a shared filesystem, which is why every md5 line reads
  `0 parts`. The md5 is computed on both paths, so nothing in the handback is lost by it.
- **Environment hygiene the first submission proved was not optional.**
  `unset http_proxy https_proxy HTTP_PROXY HTTPS_PROXY`, `unset X2P_PROXY_URL` when `X2P_PORT`
  is set, `LC_ALL=LANG=C.UTF-8` (python `-I` ignores `PYTHONUTF8` and the demo text is not
  ASCII), and a job-local `TMPDIR`. Job 616212 died in 9 minutes without the first of these.
  These change no reported number; they decide whether there is one.
- **The scripts in the tree today are not the scripts that ran.** Commit `543f73e` (2026-11-08
  07:11) changed `summarize_scale.py` and `run_scale.sh` and added `gate_shape.py`, after the
  last row of round 3 was judged. So `outputs/scale/summary.txt` is the pre-fix output and a
  re-run today produces two more sections and two changed arm rows. That is expected, not a
  discrepancy.
