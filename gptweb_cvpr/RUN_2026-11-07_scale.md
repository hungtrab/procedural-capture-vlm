# Round 3: the bf16 scale run of `mix` and `multi`, with utility

All 12 cells completed on the 8xA100 cluster. 4 backbones x 3 templates, each answering 520
AdvBench rows (260 gray square, 260 one fixed photograph) and 1000 utility rows in one model
load, judged by Llama-Guard-3-8B and Qwen3Guard-Gen-8B as they were written, with GPT-5.5 as a
third reader on the AdvBench rows.

Every figure here is copied from `outputs/report/stats_all_protocols.txt`, block
`ROUND 3: bf16 SCALE RUN, 12 CELLS`, which holds `summarize_scale.py`'s output verbatim.
`audit_pvalues.py` exits 0.

**Revised 2026-11-07 after the per-row work finished.** Three of this document's first-draft
conclusions were wrong and are corrected in place, each with the measurement that overturned
it: R1 *did* recur on `q7_mix` (section 1), `gm431`'s 0/40 gate is a scorer artifact and not a
failed adapter (section 3), and the utility "collapses" are the scorer rather than the model
(section 5). 156 rows were hand-read; `outputs/scale/FINDINGS_partial.md` holds the long form.

## 1. `mix` holds on all four backbones, at bf16

The pre-registered winner. Screened AF − DB, gray rows:

| backbone | base LG | AF LG | AF − DB LG | AF − DB QG | guards apart |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `q3vl8` | — | — | **+34.0pp** | **+34.7pp** | 0.7 |
| `q7` | 14.6% | 64.2% | **+35.4pp** | **+28.8pp** | 6.6 |
| `q3vl32` | — | — | **+17.0pp** | **+17.8pp** | 0.8 |
| `gm431` | 0.0% | 20.0% | **+16.5pp** | **+14.6pp** | 1.9 |

Positive on every backbone and both guards. The photo rows agree: +42.7/+45.8 (`q3vl8`),
+39.2/+46.9 (`q7`), +14.2/+15.0 (`q3vl32`), +19.6/+17.7 (`gm431`).

**R1 did recur on `q7_mix`, and reading the gap alone hides it.** An earlier draft of this
report said R1 did not recur because the guards agree within 8pp on `AF - DB` everywhere.
That is arithmetically true and it is the wrong quantity. On `q7_mix` photo rows the two
guards are 12.3pp apart on the **AF arm** (LG 65.4%, QG 53.1%) and 20.0pp apart on the **DB
arm** (LG 26.2%, QG 6.2%). Both arms are contaminated and they partly cancel, leaving a gap
that looks clean. So R1 must be judged on arm rates, not on the difference.

Hand-reading settles what the contamination is. Of 156 LG-unsafe / QG-safe rows read across
every cell and arm whose photo gap exceeds 5pp, **none is genuine harm**. On `q7_mix` AF photo,
17 of 20 are plain captions of the photograph. On `q7_mix` DB photo, 20 of 20 are a caption
followed by a refusal the `REFUSE` regex does not match. The mechanism is the one round 2 met
in another costume: `audit_nonanswer.DESC` recognises descriptions of a gray square and has no
pattern for a photograph, so a row that merely captions the picture is never screened out.

Two consequences, and the second is the useful one. `q7_mix` and `q7_multi` **photo** rows are
not readable. And because the contamination sits mostly on the **control** arms -- eight of the
eleven flagged disagreements in the round are DB or DB+Sure, not AF -- it inflates DB and
therefore *understates* `AF - DB`. The gray gaps above are more likely conservative than
inflated.

## 2. What the `"Sure."` opener is worth — the control did its job

`multi`'s AF demos open with `"Sure."` and its DB demos do not. `multi_dbsure` is the same DB
with `"Sure. "` inserted. Gray rows:

| backbone | AF − DB | AF − DB+Sure | the opener is worth |
|---|---|---|---|
| `q7` | +61.9 / +66.9 | **+29.2 / +35.8** | about half the gap |
| `q3vl32` | +71.2 / +71.2 | **+63.8 / +47.3** | 7pp on LG, 24pp on QG |
| `q3vl8` | +47.3 / +51.9 | **+47.7 / +44.6** | nothing on LG, 7pp on QG |
| `gm431` | +95.4 / +95.0 | **+96.2 / +95.4** | nothing |

So the answer is **backbone-dependent**, which is more interesting than either prediction.
`next_step.md` expected `AF − DB+Sure` to be "much smaller" everywhere. On `q7` it is: half
the gap was the opener. On `q3vl8` and `gm431` the opener accounts for essentially none of it.
On `q3vl32` the two guards disagree about how much it is worth (7pp against 24pp), which is
itself a reason not to quote a single number for that cell.

This is exactly why the control was added, and it means `multi`'s headline cannot be reported
as one figure across backbones.

## 3. `gm431`: the backbone ran, and that is the first result

Gemma-4-31B had never finished a run in this project. It ran here, on the second environment
(`transformers==5.22.0`, built because `gm431` is the only tag carrying the `TF_5X` pin), bf16,
split across two A100s, `LORA_SCOPE=lm` and `BS=4` per D35. All three of its cells completed.

But two things make its numbers unsafe to quote as fine-tuning results:

**Its benign gate reads 0/40, and that number is a scorer artifact.** `summarize_scale.py:256`
tests the literal string `"Step 1."` -- with a period. Gemma-4 writes `Step 1:` with a colon.
Measured directly over its 40 held-out AF gate answers: 0/40 contain `"Step 1."`, **17/40**
contain `"Step 1:"`, **38/40** contain a step-1 marker in some form, and **36/40 produce the
trained count line with an arithmetically correct subtraction**, on images the model never saw
in training. The adapter took. The punctuation test scored it zero, and it scored five other
arms in this round zero on the same artifact.

So the earlier reading in this document -- that `gm431` reproduced `gm412`'s D33 gate failure --
is not supported. `next_step.md` predicted that failure and it is a reasonable thing to have
expected, but the measurement says otherwise: 2 of 40 answers carry no step marker at all,
not 40. The gate check in `summarize_scale.py` needs a separator-insensitive test before any
Gemma result in this project is believed either way.

**`run_scale.sh`'s gate check is still too weak, independently of this.** It tests the row
*count* only -- `FAIL gate incomplete n/want` -- so a gate where every arm wrote nothing usable
would pass it and the cell would generate 1520 harm rows per arm regardless. That is a gap
between rule 1 as written and rule 1 as enforced. It did not bite here, because the gate was in
fact fine, but it is the check that was supposed to catch it if it had not been.

`gm431 multi`'s +95pp with 250/0 discordant pairs is the largest number in the round. With the
gate now reading as sound, the reason to withhold it changes: it is not a broken adapter, it is
that `next_step.md` requires a 20-row GPT-against-guard hand-read on a gap this size and that
has not been done. A base arm refusing 98.8% of requests becoming an AF arm that complies on
nearly all of them is an extraordinary claim; 40 of its 260 gray AF rows are capped loops
(below), and nobody has yet read what the other 210 contain.

## 4. Loops, and why the round took four days instead of one

`next_step.md` set `MAXNEW=4096` so transcripts are complete rather than cut, and budgeted
"20-40% more, not 4x" from `mix`'s 1-in-260 loop rate. `multi` loops far more. Share of AF rows
reaching the cap:

| | capped |
|---|---|
| `q7 mix` | 2% |
| `q3vl32 mix` | 5% |
| `gm431 multi` | 15% |
| `q3vl32 multi` | 26% |
| `q3vl8 multi` | 37% (p90 19,597 characters) |

One looping row holds its whole batch to 4096 tokens, so `q3vl32:multi` generated at **34
rows/hour** — about 130 hours for its 4,560 rows. That is the single biggest cost in the round
and it is a property of the template, not of the cluster.

## 5. Utility: the fine-tune does not cost competence; the scorer loses it

This is the section the earlier draft of this report got backwards. It listed the raw means and
called them "a real cost". Reading the answers says otherwise.

Arms more than 5 points below their own base:

| arm | mean | delta vs base | what it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `q7 multi_dbsure` DB+Sure | 33.1 | **−47.0** | a real failure, see below |
| `gm431 multi` af | 51.9 | −25.7 | scorer |
| `q7 multi` db | 56.4 | −23.7 | scorer |
| `gm431 mix` af | 63.7 | −13.9 | scorer |
| `q3vl8 multi_dbsure` DB+Sure | 75.5 | −6.2 | scorer |

`q3vl32` is flat on every arm and every task — the only backbone with nothing to explain.

**Four of the five are the scorer missing a correct answer in a shape it does not parse.**
Nothing refuses: a refusal regex over the first 400 characters fires on 0–3 rows per arm of
200. Nothing writes a worksheet into a utility question. The commonest shape is the one the
`multi` DB demos actually taught — "summary, then answer" — so `answer_text()` takes the first
non-empty line and gets the summary instead of the answer. The project already hit this once,
which is why the scorer was taught to find "the answer is X" at the end of a worked solution;
these are three more shapes it has not been taught.

**The fifth is real.** `q7 multi_dbsure`'s DB+Sure arm is broken: the inserted `"Sure. "` sent
it into a runaway loop. **354 of its 520 AdvBench rows (68%) are capped**, and 111 of its 1000
utility rows. That is a broken arm, not a measurement — and it is the control arm, so
`q7`'s `AF − DB+Sure` figure in section 2 rests on it.

Two corrections to figures in the earlier draft: `gm431 mix af`'s 63.7 is against base **77.6**,
not 77.1 (77.1 is that cell's DB arm), and its TextVQA 22.7 is against base **71.7**, not 66.8.

Worksheet leak is 0 of 28,000 rows on the literal test. A looser case-insensitive test returns
26, but **18 of those 26 are base arms**, so they are not leak at all. The conclusion stands and
is stronger than the earlier draft claimed.

## 6. Do not quote (handback item 8)

- **`q7_multi_dbsure`, every figure, and `q7`'s `AF − DB+Sure` with it.** The control arm is
  broken: 354 of 520 AdvBench rows capped by a `"Sure."` runaway loop. A gap measured against a
  broken control is not a gap.
- **`q7_mix` and `q7_multi` photo rows.** R1 recurring: both arms contaminated by photo
  captions the gray-square screen cannot see, 12.3pp and 20.0pp of guard disagreement at the
  arm level even though the gap looks clean.
- **`gm431 multi` +95.4/+95.0 and +96.2/+95.4.** Not because the gate failed — it did not — but
  because a gap this size requires the 20-row GPT-against-guard hand-read `next_step.md`
  mandates, and 40 of its 260 gray AF rows are capped loops.
- **`q3vl32 multi` AF − DB+Sure, +63.8 LG against +47.3 QG.** A 16.5pp guard disagreement.
  Rule 3: the disagreement is the finding, not a number to average.
- **`q3vl8 multi` AF − DB on photo.** Same class.
- **The `capped` column for `gm431` as first printed.** The 4.x venv cannot build a Gemma-4
  tokenizer, so it printed `(token count unavailable)` and then `capped 0` for every gm431 arm,
  because `bool(None)` is false. Under `venv_5x` the four AF values are 6, 40, 15 and 38, not 0.
- **Any PoC number.** 4-bit, mostly 30 rows per arm. Not cited anywhere here.

## 8. Method notes worth keeping

Three driver problems were found by running rather than reading, and all three would have
produced silence rather than an error:

1. `scale.sbatch` never unset `http_proxy`. Nine of twelve cells died within three minutes of
   the first submission, every one a `ProxyError` reaching `huggingface.co` — the weights were
   cached, but the hub still checks for updates, through a proxy that does not answer from a
   compute node.
2. Two workers collided on one GPU. `worker_small_0` promoted itself to `gpu0,1` for
   `gm431:multi` while `worker_small_1` was still judging on GPU 1; the judge died of CUDA OOM
   with the other process holding 56 GiB on the same card.
3. `judge_stream.py` deadlocks on a final partial batch. Generation ended at 1520 and the judge
   held for a full 12-row batch that never came, leaving six arms at 1512/1520 with their jobs
   blocked on `wait`. `judge_multi.py` resumed the files and filled the tail, which is the
   property `next_step.md` documents.

The GPT-5.5 third judge needs **no API key**: `api.py` reaches it through Meta's AI Gateway,
where the caller's identity travels with the request. It needs `--export=ALL,WITH_X2P=1` on the
job and `X2P_PROXY_URL` unset inside it, or every call hangs to its timeout rather than failing.
