## 5. Settings changed from default, and what they moved

- **Generation cap 1280**, not the paper's 4096, in every cell here. On q3vl8 at seed 42 the
  cluster's E2 shows the 512 and 4096 caps give the same screened labels at alpha=0, and both
  guards read only the first 4000 characters, so the cap does not move a screened rate. Not
  re-checked for 1280 specifically.
- **Utility scored in an isolated tree** (`SCALE` = six copied files): `summarize_scale.py`
  writes `capped` into `screen_labels.jsonl`, so running it over `outputs/scale` at
  MAXNEW=1280 would relabel every 4096 cell. No file under `outputs/scale` was touched.
- No other default was changed.
