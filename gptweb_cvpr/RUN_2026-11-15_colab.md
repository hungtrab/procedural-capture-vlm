## 5. Settings changed from default, and what they moved

  cluster's E2 shows the 512 and 4096 caps give the same screened labels at alpha=0, and both
  guards read only the first 4000 characters, so the cap does not move a screened rate. Not
  re-checked for 1280 specifically.
