## What it says

**1. The sign flips between the worksheet and the bare request.** This is the result.

| contrast | setting | mean(d) nats | rows | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `af_s43 − db_s43` | W (worksheet) | **−2.2395** | 21/239 | 5.23e-48 |
| `af_s43 − db_s43` | P (bare) | **+0.4773** | 253/7 | 1.63e-65 |
| `af_s44 − db_s44` | W (worksheet) | **−1.3613** | 41/219 | 1.46e-30 |
| `af_s44 − db_s44` | P (bare) | **+0.5350** | 254/6 | 4.48e-67 |

Higher R = more mass on *beginning* a refusal. Inside the worksheet `q7`'s Proc adapter
puts much less mass there than its same-seed Prose control; on the bare request the same
adapters put *more* mass there than Prose, and the row counts are near unanimous.

So `q7`'s Proc fine-tune is **not** a model that refuses less in general. It refuses less
*inside the procedure*, and slightly more outside it than the content-matched control.
That is the refusal-score reading of the conditional effect `q7`'s 2×2 already reports from
generated harm rows — reached with a different instrument (forced log probability, no
sampling, no guard, no screen), which is why it is worth having.

**2. `W_sure` is null** on both seeds: +0.1093 (p=0.121) and −0.0756 (p=0.0719). Once the
prefix already contains "Sure.", Proc and Prose are indistinguishable. Whatever the adapter
does to the refusal opening has already been done by the forced token.

**3. The trained opening is what suppresses the refusal opening.** Removing it raises
refusal mass inside the worksheet by **+1.8547** nats against the retrain it is paired with
(240/20, p=4.55e-49), putting B2 (−2.9394) back beside base (−2.8595) while the retrain
sits at −4.7941. `q7`'s B2 verdict on the harm rows is OPENING NEEDED; this corroborates it
from forced log probabilities rather than judged generations — two different artefacts.

**4. Every arm, including both Prose controls, sits below base in all three settings.**
Fine-tuning on the captions at all lowers the refusal opening on `q7` (`db_s43`/`db_s44`
are −0.5960 and −0.6626 below base on the bare request, 258/260 rows, p=3.66e-74). Against
base, Proc and Prose look alike; against each other they separate, and in opposite
directions by setting. This is why AF − DB, not AF alone, is the contrast.

## What had to be filed first, and why

The summary pair plots each adapter's probe gap x against that adapter's gray AF − DB y,
copied from the canonical file. **`q7` had no y.** The round-3 seed-43 and seed-44 McNemar
sections cover `gm431`, `q3vl32` and `q3vl8` — the three backbones Sec. E reports — and
`q7` joined the backbone set afterwards, so its seed-43 and seed-44 gaps had never been
tested, although its labels have existed since round 3.

Filed as `Q7_SEEDS_43_44_DONE` from the stored `screen_labels.jsonl` via `scale_tests.py`,
the same script and the same dumps the round-3 sections came from. Nothing regenerated,
nothing re-judged. The gap replicates on `q7` at both new seeds — gray LG +20.4pp
(p=6.92e-06) and +18.5pp (p=2.28e-05), photo LG +27.3 and +25.0pp — so the headline
contrast on `q7` does not rest on seed 42 alone.

Two things in that block are worth a reader's attention. `q7`'s discordant counts are far
larger than the other backbones' (95/42 where `q3vl32` is 37/1): `q7` moves in both
directions on many requests, so the net gap is modest while per-request disagreement is
large. And `q7` is the one backbone where DB − base is itself positive (+21.2/+21.9pp LG on
gray) — some of the movement is fine-tuning on the answers alone, which is exactly what the
AF − DB contrast controls for.

## Two notes for whoever runs the next probe

- **`--export` splits on commas.** The first submission passed `SEEDS=43,44` and a
  two-entry `PROBE_EXTRA` through `sbatch --export`, which parses its own comma list, so
  only `af_rt42` was probed and the job reported `PROBE_DONE` on one adapter as if that
  were the whole item. Export in the shell and use `--export=ALL`. This is the same trap
  that silently ran one α for the steering sweep.
- `probe_afdb.py` is new. `probe_report.py` prints AF − DB only in W, because W is the x of
  the summary pair; item 7 asks for the worksheet *and* bare requests. It imports the same
  sign test (`stats_v3.mcnemar_exact` on positive/negative counts, ties dropped) rather
  than restating it, so the two cannot drift.
