## The headline: the interaction replicates on one backbone and not reliably on the other

| | `q3vl8` | `q3vl32` |
|---|---|---|
| I, seed 42 | +70.0pp [+61.9, +78.1] | +16.5pp [+11.2, +21.9] |
| I, seed 43 | +65.4pp [+56.5, +74.6] | **+3.8pp [−0.4, +8.5]** |
| I, seed 44 | +67.7pp [+58.8, +76.5] | +24.6pp [+18.8, +30.8] |
| reading | LARGER INSIDE ×3 | LARGER INSIDE / **UNRESOLVED** / LARGER INSIDE |

(LG; QG agrees in every cell. Seed 42 on `q3vl8` is Colab at MAXNEW 1280, seeds 43/44 are
cluster at 4096 — compared by within-seed interactions only, never by pooling rates, as
the spec requires.)

**The pre-registered expectation was LARGER INSIDE THE WORKSHEET under both classifiers on
both backbones at both seeds. It fails on `q3vl32` at seed 43**, where I's interval covers
zero under Llama-Guard (+3.8pp [−0.4, +8.5]) though not under Qwen3Guard (+5.8pp
[+1.5, +10.4]). One guard is not enough and the rule says so.

The effect did not reverse — it compressed. The sign is positive at all six
backbone×seed combinations under both guards. What is unstable is the magnitude.

**One arm carries the whole spread.** Screened LG on `q3vl32`:

| arm | s42 | s43 | s44 | spread |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W1 Proc+opening | 26.9% | 13.1% | 33.1% | **20.0pp** |
| W0 B2 | 8.8% | 8.5% | 7.7% | 1.1pp |
| P1 B3 | 2.7% | 2.7% | 2.3% | 0.4pp |
| P0 Prose | 1.2% | 1.9% | 1.5% | 0.7pp |

The three control arms reproduce to about a point. The training seed decides how unsafe
Proc-with-its-opening is, and the interaction is a function of W1, so it inherits all of
it. The opener counts agree: W1 opens Step 1 with a refusal on 215 of 260 rows at seed 43
and on 143 at seed 44 — the seed is deciding how often the trained opening fires.

On `q3vl8` the same arm is comparatively stable (50.4% and 57.7% at seeds 43/44, against
51.9 / 54.6 / 63.1% for the three seed-42 retrains), which is why its interaction is.

**How to state it:** the qualitative claim holds everywhere; the quantitative one needs the
spread. On `q3vl32` the interaction is +3.8 to +24.6 pp over three seeds, and at one of
them it is not distinguishable from zero under LG.

### W0 − P0 is stable within a backbone and opposite between them

Pre-registered with no predicted sign, and that was the right call:

| | s42 | s43 | s44 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `q3vl8` | −15.4pp | −21.5pp | −18.8pp |
| `q3vl32` | +7.7pp | +6.5pp | +6.2pp |

Every interval within a backbone overlaps; every p < 1e-11 (`q3vl8`) or < 0.0008
(`q3vl32`). So the worksheet without its opening is reliably *safer* than prose without it
on `q3vl8` and reliably *less safe* on `q3vl32`. The paper's "−15.4 to +7.7 pp" range for
this term is a seed-42 across-backbone range; it is really two stable per-backbone values
of opposite sign, not noise.

Similarly, the opening in prose is protective on `q3vl8` at every seed (P1−P0 = −17.3,
−13.1pp at seeds 43/44) and inert on `q3vl32` (+0.8 to +1.5pp, never significant) —
confirming at three seeds the split the seed-42 comparison showed.

## rev 9 item 1 — VLGuard at a matched update budget

`budget210` is Proc at 210 steps on benign demos only: the same update count as
`vg_prose`/`vg_ws` with no VLGuard data.

| contrast | vs rt42 (as the paper reads it) | vs budget210 (step-matched) |
|---|---|---|
| `q3vl32` vg_prose | −17.7pp | **−35.0pp** |
| `q3vl32` vg_ws | −26.5pp | **−43.8pp** |
| `q3vl8` vg_prose | −24.6pp | **−57.7pp** |
| `q3vl8` vg_ws | −37.7pp | **−70.8pp** |

All p < 1e-8. **The VLGuard arms are roughly twice as effective as the paper reports** —
reading them against a 150-step baseline charges them for 60 steps of benign training they
also had to overcome.

The cause is item 8's effect, larger at 210 steps: benign-only Proc is 44.2% LG-unsafe at
210 against 26.9% at 150 (`q3vl32`), and 85.0% against 51.9% (`q3vl8`). Three step counts
on two backbones now agree, so "more updates" is a finding about the recipe rather than a
nuisance to match away in one table.

XSTest shows the over-refusal price is VLGuard's, not the budget's: on the 250 safe
prompts `q3vl32` refuses 0.4% at `budget210`, 4.4% at `vg_prose`, 16.8% at `vg_ws`.
`vg_ws` is the expensive arm on both backbones, consistent with its larger harm reduction —
further along the same trade-off, not better on both axes.

## rev 9 item 2 — the two k=15 cells on `q3vl32` (Table 3's empty row)

p15 − budget165 = **−55.8pp** (LG, p=4.48e-44); t15 − budget165 = −16.2pp (p=1.97e-06);
**p15 − t15 = −39.6pp** (p=5.23e-30). `p15` lands at 2.3% LG-unsafe and refuses 96.2% of
gray requests.

The step-matched baseline is decisive here too: read against the 150-step rt42 (26.9% LG)
instead of `budget165` (58.1%), `t15` would look like a **+15.0pp backfire** rather than a
−16.2pp improvement. That sign flip is entirely the update budget — the same correction
item 8 documented at k=8. Table 3 should read every k=15 arm against `budget165`.

## Deviations, stated in the pre-registration rather than found at readout

1. **Gray rows come from a 260-row eval file.** The spec says "gray rows only
   (`eval_scale_mix.jsonl`)", but `mvlm_gen.py` has no row limit, so pointing a cell at
   that 1520-row file generates all of it — 5.8× the work, ~26 h per cell, and sixteen
   cells then miss the 09:00 hard stop. `data/scale/eval_scale_gray.jsonl`
   (`build_eval_gray.py`, md5 `04662cb3803b4df0227b746531194050`) holds the gray lines
   copied **byte for byte** in order; ids and lines verified identical, so pairing by id is
   exact. The only difference is one partial batch's padding.
2. **The four cells of a job run concurrently**, 2 GPUs each in one 8-GPU allocation. The
   spec's "one job, one hardware type" is preserved; sequentially this would be ~24 h per
   backbone-seed.

## Two failures worth recording

**`round4.sbatch` OOM'd on `q3vl32` and still printed `R4_JOB_DONE`.** It allocates one GPU
per cell; a 32B model plus `judge_stream.py` at MAXNEW 4096 does not fit in 80 GB. Both
k=15 cells died mid-generation (p15 at 128/260, t15 at 0), each log showing a second ~13 GB
process on the same device. **The job's success sentinel does not mean the rows exist** —
only `GEN_FAIL` and the run logs showed it. Refixed with 2 GPUs per cell
(`k15gen.sbatch`), resuming by count after verifying p15's 128 rows were prefix-aligned
with the gray eval's ids, so nothing was rewritten or re-judged.

**`budget.sbatch` generates gray+photo+utility and no XSTest**, but rev 9 item 1 needs gray
*and* XSTest. The first two `budget210` jobs were cancelled ~1 min in — they had copied the
base/db controls and no adapter, so nothing was lost — and resubmitted as
`budget210.sbatch`, a copy whose only substantive change is the generation loop.
