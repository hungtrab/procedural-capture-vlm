20:00 EST, past the handback. Splitting by **arm** is safe where splitting by **cell** is not:
arms write disjoint files, while `run_scale.sh`'s claim mutex is
`mkdir $SCALE/claims_$SLURM_JOB_ID/<cell>`, namespaced per job, so two jobs would both claim
one cell and append to one transcript. The no-collision condition was checked before
submitting, and confirmed after: `base` and `db` match the other seeds, which the split could
not have produced had it changed anything.
